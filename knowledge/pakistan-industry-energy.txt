# Pakistan special economic zones, gas infrastructure and mining
# One fact per line, phrased with the words someone would ask with.

Rashakai SEZ in Nowshera, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, is a CPEC special economic zone for high-tech, textile processing, mechanical equipment, appliances and pharmaceuticals, with M-1 motorway access and its own 132 kV grid station.
Allama Iqbal Industrial City, AIIC, in Faisalabad, Punjab, spans over 3,217 acres beside the M-4 motorway and targets textiles, engineering, electronics, chemicals and food processing.
Dhabeji SEZ in Thatta, Sindh, is 50 km from Port Qasim and targets steel foundries, automotive assembly, petrochemicals and heavy engineering.
Bostan SEZ in Pishin, Balochistan, is near Quetta and targets fruit processing, cold storage, agricultural machinery and halal food, linking trade routes to Afghanistan and Central Asia via Chaman.

SNGPL, Sui Northern Gas Pipelines Limited, carries gas to Punjab, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa and Islamabad through high-pressure transmission loop lines.
SSGC, Sui Southern Gas Company, serves Sindh and Balochistan and links onshore fields such as Sui and Bhit to the gas grid.
The Engro Elengy Terminal, EETL, at Port Qasim in Karachi is a floating storage and regasification unit, FSRU, with a peak LNG capacity of 600 mmcfd.
The Gasport LNG terminal at Port Qasim is Pakistan's second LNG terminal, providing regasification up to 600 mmcfd to ease winter gas shortages.

The Saindak copper-gold project in Chagai district, Balochistan, is an open-pit mine and blister copper plant with its own thermal power unit.
Thar Coal Blocks II and VI in the Thar desert in Sindh produce lignite coal for mine-mouth power plants under CPEC, replacing imported fuel oil.
Tuwairqi Steel Mills, TSML, in the Port Qasim corridor is Pakistan's first private-sector direct reduced iron, DRI, plant for high-grade steel.
