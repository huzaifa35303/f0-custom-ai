# Pakistan Knowledge Overview
# One fact per line. Lines starting with # are ignored.
# Each line is phrased with the words someone would ask the question with,
# because recall matches on shared words.

The official name of Pakistan is the Islamic Republic of Pakistan.
The capital of Pakistan is Islamabad.
The founder of Pakistan is Quaid-e-Azam Muhammad Ali Jinnah.
Pakistan gained independence on 14 August 1947.
The currency of Pakistan is the Pakistani Rupee, PKR.
The official languages of Pakistan are Urdu and English.
The President of Pakistan is Asif Ali Zardari.
The Prime Minister of Pakistan is Muhammad Shehbaz Sharif.

The largest province of Pakistan by area is Balochistan.
The highest peak in Pakistan is K2, at 8,616 metres, in the Karakoram range.
The longest river in Pakistan is the Indus River, about 2,896 km long, and it is the lifeline of the country.
The largest desert in Pakistan is the Thar Desert, followed by the Cholistan and Thal deserts.
The major glaciers of Pakistan are the Siachen Glacier, Baltoro and Batura.
The largest dam in Pakistan is the Tarbela Dam.

The national sport of Pakistan is field hockey.
The national flower of Pakistan is the jasmine.
The national animal of Pakistan is the markhor.
The national bird of Pakistan is the chukar partridge, also called the chakor.
The national tree of Pakistan is the deodar cedar.
The national poet of Pakistan is Allama Iqbal.
The national anthem of Pakistan was written by Hafeez Jullundhri.

The first Nobel Prize winner of Pakistan was Dr Abdus Salam, for Physics in 1979.
The first female Prime Minister of Pakistan was Benazir Bhutto.
The first Governor-General of Pakistan was Muhammad Ali Jinnah.
The first Prime Minister of Pakistan was Liaquat Ali Khan.
The first capital of Pakistan was Karachi.
