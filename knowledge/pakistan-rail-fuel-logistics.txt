# Pakistan railways, emergency communications, fuel pipelines and dry ports
# From the encyclopedic vault. Only what the other files do not already cover.
# One fact per line, phrased with the words someone would ask with.

Pakistan Railways main lines ML-1, ML-2 and ML-3 run on 1,676 mm broad gauge, while 762 mm narrow gauge survives only as legacy mountain tourist track.
Pakistan Railways signalling mixes modern computer based interlocking, CBI, with older mechanical rod-and-cable frames, and automatic block signalling protects busy urban sections.
Pakistan Railways runs diesel-electric freight locomotives including the American GE Evolution series of 4,000 horsepower and Chinese CRRC units.

The NDMA disaster communications network uses dedicated radio frequencies and satellite relays to keep contact when commercial cell towers fail during a disaster.
Pakistan's emergency response trucks carry high-power HF and VHF radio sets giving first responders encrypted voice and telemetry links.
Automatic weather monitors and river sensors send real-time data by satellite to central command, powering early warning for floods, cloudbursts and earthquakes in Pakistan.

The White Oil Pipeline, WOPL, is an 864 km pipeline carrying high-speed diesel and petrol from Pakistan's coastal terminals to inland depots, replacing thousands of tanker trucks.
The Keamari to Mahmood Kot crude pipeline, KMK, is an 870 km pipeline pumping imported crude oil from Karachi port to refineries in central Pakistan.
The Machaike terminal in Punjab is a fuel distribution hub handling pipeline receipts, fuel quality testing and bulk truck loading for northern markets.

The NLC dry port in Lahore is an inland customs and freight depot letting central Punjab businesses clear international cargo before it moves to the seaports.
The Sost dry port on the Karakoram Highway near the China border is the world's highest dry port, handling trade through the Karakoram pass in season.
The Sialkot Dry Port Trust is a privately built and run dry port offering automated customs clearance and container stuffing for surgical instrument and sports goods exports.
