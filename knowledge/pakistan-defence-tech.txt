# Pakistan naval fleet, nuclear power, space programme and digital economy
# One fact per line, phrased with the words someone would ask with.

Hangor-class submarines are 8 diesel-electric attack submarines with Air-Independent Propulsion, built with China, the first 4 in China and the remaining 4 at Karachi Shipyard, and they carry the Babur-3 cruise missile.
Tughril-class frigates, Type 054A/P, are 4 Chinese-built guided-missile stealth frigates carrying LY-80N anti-air and CM-302 supersonic anti-ship missiles, and form the backbone of the Pakistan Navy surface fleet.
Babur-class corvettes, from the Turkish Milgem class, are 4 multi-mission stealth corvettes with local Harbah-NG combat systems and anti-submarine warfare capability.
Jinnah-class frigates are an indigenous Pakistani frigate programme developed jointly with Turkey following technology transfer from the Milgem initiative.

The Karachi Nuclear Power Plant, KANUPP, runs reactors K-2 and K-3 with a total net capacity of 2,200 MW using HPR1000 Hualong One pressurised water reactors.
The Chashma Nuclear Power Plant, CHASNUPP, runs four CNP-300 pressurised water reactors with a total of 1,330 MW, with Chashma-5 planned to add a 1,200 MW Hualong One unit.
Pakistan's civil nuclear power is regulated by the Pakistan Nuclear Regulatory Authority, PNRA, and operated by the Pakistan Atomic Energy Commission, PAEC.

SUPARCO is the Space and Upper Atmosphere Research Commission, Pakistan's space agency, established in 1961 by Nobel laureate Dr Abdus Salam.
Pakistan was the first South Asian country to launch a sounding rocket, Rehbar-I, in 1962.
PakSat-1R is a Pakistani communications satellite launched in 2011 into geostationary orbit at 38 degrees east, carrying broadband, TV broadcasting and emergency telecommunications.
PRSS-1, the Pakistan Remote Sensing Satellite, was launched in 2018 into sun-synchronous orbit for high-resolution earth observation, mapping and border surveillance.
PakTES-1A is a technology evaluation satellite launched in 2018 into low earth orbit to verify locally built sensors and satellite bus designs.
iCube-Qamar is Pakistan's first lunar cubesat, launched in May 2024 on China's Chang'e 6 mission to image the lunar surface and gather deep-space telemetry.

Pakistan's IT exports and freelance sector are supported by the Pakistan Software Export Board, PSEB, and its special technology zones, and rank among the top global providers of software development.
Raast is the State Bank of Pakistan's instant, zero-cost digital payment system for real-time person-to-person and person-to-business transfers.
NayaPay and Sadapay are Pakistani Electronic Money Institutions, EMIs, part of a move to branchless digital banking under newly issued digital banking licences.
